# Hi, I'm Fatima Ezzahra Barknan 👋

**Agricultural engineer & data scientist** — State Engineer in Agronomy, option *Data Science in Agriculture* (IAV Hassan II, Rabat, 2026).

I use machine learning and Earth observation data to answer agronomic and environmental questions: how much water crops really use, how yields respond to climate, and where species and ecosystems will be able to persist under climate change.


---

### 🔬 Research interests
- Crop yield prediction and agronomic modelling
- Remote sensing of crops and water use (evapotranspiration, water productivity)
- Machine learning / deep learning for agriculture and the environment
- Species distribution modelling and climate change scenarios (CMIP6)

### 📂 Featured projects

| Project | What it does | Tools |
|---|---|---|
| [**irrigation-wapor-moulay-bousselham**](https://github.com/fatimaezzahra-barknan/irrigation-wapor-moulay-bousselham) | Crop water use and irrigation efficiency in an irrigated area of northern Morocco over 7 seasons (2018–2025), from FAO WaPOR v3 data: water productivity, irrigation supply, and K-means typology of fields | Python, WaPOR, K-means |
| [**plant-disease-detection**](https://github.com/fatimaezzahra-barknan/plant-disease-detection) | Leaf disease classifier (38 classes) with a fine-tuned ResNet18 — 98.5 % test accuracy, deployed as a web app with in-browser inference | PyTorch, ONNX, Gradio, Hugging Face |
| **Atlas cedar distribution under climate change** *(final-year thesis, 19/20)* | Habitat modelling of *Cedrus atlantica* in Morocco with MaxEnt, random forest and gradient boosting, spatial block cross-validation, ensemble model and projections under 36 CMIP6 combinations | R |
| **Wheat yield prediction** *(INRA Rabat / academic project)* | Prediction of durum and soft wheat yields at province and commune level (Isolation Forest, random forest, XGBoost), and analysis of climate–yield relationships at INRA | Python, scikit-learn |
