### Featured projects
💧 **[irrigation-wapor-moulay-bousselham](https://github.com/fatimaezzahra-barknan/irrigation-wapor-moulay-bousselham)**
Crop water use and irrigation efficiency in an irrigated area of northern Morocco over 7 seasons (2018–2025), from FAO WaPOR v3 data — water productivity, irrigation supply and K-means typology of fields.
*Python · WaPOR · K-means*

🍃 **[plant-disease-detection](https://github.com/fatimaezzahra-barknan/plant-disease-detection)**
Leaf disease classifier (38 classes) with a fine-tuned ResNet18 — **98.5 % test accuracy** — deployed as a web app with in-browser inference.
*PyTorch · ONNX · Gradio · Hugging Face Spaces*

🌲 **Atlas cedar under climate change** — *final-year thesis, 19/20*
Habitat modelling of *Cedrus atlantica* in Morocco with MaxEnt, random forest and gradient boosting, spatial block cross-validation and projections under 36 CMIP6 scenarios.
*R · species distribution modelling*

🌾 **Wheat yield prediction** — *INRA Rabat / academic project*
Prediction of durum and soft wheat yields at province and commune level and analysis of climate–yield relationships.
*Python · scikit-learn · XGBoost*
